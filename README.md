# Green Horizon Landing

Create a landing page replicating the provided design screenshot in full detail. Implement the exact visual hierarchy, colors (lush greens, warm orange, soft neutrals), typography, and layout:

1. Navigation bar: logo, navigation links, and CTA button.
2. Hero section: full-width rural landscape imagery with dark gradient overlay, headline ("A resilient future calls for behaviour change today"), subtitle, and CTA.
3. Three pillar icons: Research, Communication, and Capacity/Action with distinct geometric icons and pastel/accent color badges.
4. Mission statement & impact metric counters: '13+ years', '100+ projects', '10+ states in India' with animated numbers and photo textures.
5. Interactive Sector Explorer ("Working across multiple sectors"): interactive sidebar tabs (Agriculture, Climate, Biodiversity, Clean Energy, Health & Sanitation, Economy) switching rich illustration/preview cards.
6. Feature spotlight section: full-width green banner with field story image, quote, and button.
7. Initiatives & Case Studies grid: interactive cards ('Gamified Learning', 'Storytelling for Change', 'Biodiversity Through Theatre', 'Capturing Field Stories') with category badges, author tags, and video play hover triggers.
8. Team & community photo highlight section with orange accent card.
9. Insights & Knowledge Hub: report/resource preview cards with links.
10. Comprehensive footer: newsletter subscription, sitemap links, social icons, and branding.

Use Framer Motion for scroll-triggered fades, card hover tilts/elevations, smooth tab transitions, and animated stat counters. Keep clean modular component architecture with React Router.
